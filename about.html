OUR STORY

Seasoned expertise, applied honestly.

Standard Practice Professional brings together a team of seasoned, highly qualified experts across Audit and Assurance, Taxation, Consulting Services, Actuarial Services, Advisory, and Accounting Services.

Our partners carry over twenty-five years of cumulative experience, distinguishing themselves across agriculture, healthcare, energy, manufacturing, financial services, oil and gas, technology and telecommunications, and real estate, among others.

That experience now adds up to more than 700 completed engagements — and a client base, both local and international, that continues to recommend us, a trust we never take for granted.
