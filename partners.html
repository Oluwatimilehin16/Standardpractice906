Kolawole Ogunbowale

Operational Partner & Co-Founder
FCA MBA — Lagos Business School ICAN Fellow CITN Fellow

Kolawole is a highly accomplished and results-driven Chartered Accountant with extensive experience in finance, taxation, audit, and accounting practices, known for driving operational efficiency and delivering accurate, insightful financial information that supports strategic decision-making.

He holds a BSc in Accounting from Olabisi Onabanjo University and an MBA from Lagos Business School, and is a Fellow of both the Institute of Chartered Accountants of Nigeria (ICAN) and the Chartered Institute of Taxation of Nigeria (CITN). He began his career with Sada, Idris & Co. in Abuja before joining Goldwyns Nigeria in 2012.

In 2012, Kolawole co-founded Standard Practice Professionals, initially as Consultant and Review Manager, transitioning into a full-time role as Operational Partner in December 2016. He currently focuses on audit engagements, professional training, regulatory compliance, and guidance on complex financial accounting issues under IFRS.

YA

Oluwayemisi Kadijat Adeleke

Consultant — Macroeconomics & Financial Economics
PhD Economics ACCA Associate Senior Lecturer

Yemisi is a Senior Lecturer at Redeemer's University, Nigeria, teaching undergraduate and postgraduate students across Macroeconomics, Development Economics, Environmental Economics and Financial Economics, with recent research interests in sustainable development goals, governance and the green economy, and migration and remittances.

She joined Standard Practice Professional as a consultant focused on Macroeconomics and Financial Economics research, driving industry economic consultation and operational improvement tailored to clients' specific needs.

She received a fully sponsored PhD scholarship from the African Economic Research Consortium (AERC), a collaborative programme between the University of Ibadan and University of Benin, and holds an MSc in Economics from the University of Ibadan and a BSc in Economics from Covenant University. She is an Associate member of the Association of Chartered Certified Accountants (ACCA) and was part of the team that won the CODESRIA 2023/2024 Meaning-making Research Initiative (MRI) grant.
